# Executable analysis endpoints

The authoritative declarations live in each family's `lane/knowledge/endpoints.yaml`, `space.yaml` and `rungs.yaml`. The generated ladders include every context requirement, assumption, decision trajectory, execution capability and terminal failure:

- [Adjustment](ladders/adjustment.md): twelve executable endpoints plus diagnostic ID expressions.
- [Difference in differences](ladders/diff_in_diff.md): six structural endpoints with explicit pooled, horizon and cohort targets.
- [Discontinuity](ladders/discontinuity.md): six combinations of sharp/fuzzy/eligibility target and continuity/local-randomisation framework.

Every lane separates reasoning, engine adapters, execution and artifacts. The desk admits context; a lane settles its design obligations and returns a typed deficiency when essential context was omitted. Frozen specifications and design-qualified addresses prevent result records from conflating different populations or inference choices. A declared capability remains conditional on its causal assumptions.

The finite supported expansion excludes continuous dose and multiple treatment adjustment, complex mediation, generic CATE learners, arbitrary ID-expression estimation, GCM tasks, treatment reversals, nonlinear or survey-weighted DiD, irregular-period LP comparisons, regression kink, multiple-cutoff RD and clustered local-randomisation inference. These are explicit unsupported configurations.
