## Shared boundaries and artifact fidelity

Admission rejects model-sourced confirmation and unauthorized missing/contradictory requirements. Context identities include actual dataset bytes and versions. Authorization preserves the displayed preview exactly. Corrections and suppositions prepare a new snapshot and require a new word to run. Suppositions update an isolated graph-state copy and never save it as durable dataset memory.

All lanes have separate reasoning, engine, execution and artifact modules, enforced by twelve import contracts. Three gate attempts, one declared pre-freeze revisit, eight executable specifications and sixteen adjustment structural combinations are explicit bounds. Essential overflow is a recorded limitation. Source proposals and every revision/episode visit remain inspectable. Lead selection records target alignment, assumption/support evidence, catalogue rank and stable identity before target-effect inspection; failed leads and every failed or missing primary contrast remain visible. Stratification that would trim the frozen population is excluded before fitting, and any unexpected clipped fit or bootstrap draw refuses the estimate. A child-process failure produces a typed execution failure with its authorized identity and attempt record.

Result/API/frontend records carry `designs`, `lead_design_id`, context, ledger, candidate/episode histories, attempts, comparisons, separate falsifications/sensitivities and qualified addresses. Numerical comparisons require matching target, population, contrast, scale, support and weights. There is no universal agreement cutoff. Canonical `result.json` is downloadable; obsolete singular-design exports and old compatibility records are removed.

Unrestricted pre-freeze exploration always removes the outcome, including outcome-only requests and treatment proxies such as scores, instruments and adoption metadata. Deterministic profile summaries remain available. Actual column-access checks distinguish a missing outcome named `y` from plot-axis labels or pyplot imports. A failed analysis without a frozen design cannot lift the fence. Exploration uses separate script-step and final-record prompts, and malformed records get bounded visible refusals.

## Explicit unsupported scope

Continuous-dose or multiple-treatment adjustment, complex mediation, arbitrary individual/CATE learners, arbitrary ID-expression estimation and GCM tasks remain unsupported. DiD excludes treatment reversals, nonlinear/survey-weighted estimation and irregular LP comparisons. RD excludes regression kink, multiple cutoffs and clustered local randomisation. These configurations cannot be silently substituted with another estimand. Statistical checks never establish every causal assumption.
