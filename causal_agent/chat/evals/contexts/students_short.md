Each row is one student's results from the May 2026 exam at one school; every student who sat is included, absentees are not.
The school ran a six-week test preparation course before the exam. The counsellor offered places first to students on free or
reduced lunch and to students whose parents hold no degree; remaining places were open to anyone who asked. Completion was
recorded by the counsellor.
gender: recorded at enrolment.
race/ethnicity: the school's own grouping, letters A to E, recorded at enrolment.
parental level of education: highest qualification held by either parent, declared at enrolment.
lunch: standard or free/reduced, assigned by the district from household income before the exam.
test preparation course: completed or none, per the counsellor's record.
math score, reading score, writing score: the exam marks, 0 to 100, recorded at the sitting.
