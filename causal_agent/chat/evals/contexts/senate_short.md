Each row is one US Senate election, a state and a year, 1914 to 2010. Every election in the period with the needed returns is in the file.
A Democrat who wins the seat holds it for six years. The score is the Democratic margin of victory at that election in percentage
points; at or above zero the Democrat won, below zero the Democrat lost. Nothing records receipt separately: winning is the change.
state: the state name.
year: year of the election.
margin: Democratic margin of victory at the election, in points, minus 100 to 100.
vote: Democratic vote share at the next election for the same seat six years later.
class: Senate class of the seat, 1 to 3, which fixes the years it is contested.
termshouse, termssenate: terms served in the House and the Senate as of the election.
dopen: 1 if the seat was open, no incumbent running.
population: state population in the election year.
presdemvoteshlag1: Democratic vote share in the state at the presidential election before this one.
demvoteshlag1, demvoteshlag2: Democratic vote share at the state's previous one and two Senate elections.
demvoteshfor1: Democratic vote share at the state's next Senate election two years later, for the other seat.
demwinprv1, demwinprv2: 1 if the Democrat won the previous one and two Senate elections.
dmidterm: 1 if a midterm year. dpresdem: 1 if the sitting president was a Democrat.
