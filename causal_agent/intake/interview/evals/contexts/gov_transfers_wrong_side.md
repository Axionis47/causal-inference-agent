Each row is one household from a 2007 survey in Uruguay, taken after the PANES cash transfer programme of 2005 to 2007. The file keeps
only households whose eligibility score sits within a narrow band around the programme's cutoff, drawn two eligible households for
every ineligible one. The government paid a monthly transfer to poor households. Eligibility came from a score predicted from household
characteristics before the programme; households with a score above zero got the transfer and households below did not.
Income_Centered: the eligibility score minus the cutoff, so the cutoff is at zero, computed before the programme.
Education: years of schooling of the household head, a characteristic fixed before the programme.
Age: age of the household head, fixed before the programme.
Participation: 1 if the household received the transfer, 0 if not.
Support: the respondent's stated support for the government in the 2007 survey, scored 0, 0.5, or 1.
