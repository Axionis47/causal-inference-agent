## What changed
**A six-week test preparation course.** It reached students, before the May 2026 exam. [user, 2026-09-16] Units chose whether to take the change: The counsellor offered places first to students on free or reduced lunch and to students whose parents hold no degree; remaining places were open to anyone who asked. Students then chose whether to take the course. test preparation course records who got it, 'completed' meaning yes. A unit could change what the rule looked at after seeing it. In the file: 'test preparation course' takes 2 values. [profile] [user, 2026-09-16]
